# UniBoat: cooling-loop model dimensions

`uniboat_cooling.blend` contains an open-deck cutaway so the cooling path is visible. The [Monaco Energy Boat Challenge Energy Class](https://energyboatchallenge.com/challenge/participants/energy-class/) supplies a standard catamaran hull and limits stored energy to 10 kWh. No hull drawing or UniBoat component CAD is present in this repository, so the 10 × 4.4 m hull, foil geometry and all hardware envelopes are **estimates**.

| Component | Simulation or sizing basis | Blender envelope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1233zd(E) phase change | CoolProp at 35 °C: 185.78 kJ/kg latent heat, 1.84 bar absolute saturation, ≈1238 kg/m³ saturated liquid density | No depicted liquid height; colour denotes thermal telemetry only |
| Peak refrigerant flow | 11.59 kW ÷ (185.8 kJ/kg × 0.58 target quality) = 0.1076 kg/s; ≈5.21 L/min saturated liquid | 12 mm assumed line ID, 16 mm displayed OD; liquid velocity ≈0.77 m/s |
| Pump | 5.21 L/min, 7.5 W electrical at peak. At assumed 30% overall efficiency, available pressure rise ≈25.9 kPa | 0.15 m diameter × 0.18 m can, estimated; no selected pump curve |
| Battery and cold plate | 5.55 kW battery heat at peak. Competition 10 kWh energy cap; 120 Wh/L assumed pack density requires 83.3 L | 0.75 × 0.45 × 0.25 m battery casing = 84.4 L; 0.75 × 0.45 × 0.03 m plate |
| Motor jacket | 5.62 kW motor heat at peak | Motor 0.32 m diameter × 0.40 m, jacket 0.38 m diameter × 0.30 m; estimates |
| SiC inverter | ≈0.42 kW heat at peak | 0.32 × 0.22 × 0.13 m enclosure and 0.34 × 0.24 × 0.03 m plate; estimates |
| Seawater condenser | Approximately 11.6 kW rejection. At 5 K seawater rise, estimated water flow is 0.555 kg/s or 33.3 L/min | 0.30 × 0.18 × 0.38 m plate pack; 28 mm displayed seawater line OD, estimates |
| Accumulator | Refrigerant charge and start-up transient unspecified | 0.26 m diameter × 0.50 m shell, estimated |

At 35 °C refrigerant saturation and 37.2 °C modelled battery peak, the battery-to-refrigerant temperature difference is only 2.2 K. Rejecting 5.55 kW would require a total thermal resistance below about 0.40 K/kW. The displayed plate dimensions alone do **not** establish that performance; channel design and thermal contact need validation.

The viewer's speed, heat and temperature traces are algebraic illustrative trajectories generated by `make_uniboat.py`. The pump channel follows the algebraic flow rule and is not output from a PID controller. The 3D state colours follow the selected time sample; the viewer does not claim a solved boiling field.

Edit `uniboat_cooling.blend` in Blender, preserve the part names used by `uniboat_scene.js`, export to `uniboat_cooling.glb`, regenerate with `python build_all.py`, and inspect with `run_viewer.cmd`.
