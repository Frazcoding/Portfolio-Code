## Defect found and fixed: winter tomato price

DEFRA wholesale records UK-grown produce only. Round tomato has no Jan-Feb data and one March observation (GBP 3.00/kg); `cashflow.defra_monthly` back-filled Jan-Mar from that single value. Months with fewer than 3 observations now take the Comtrade import CIF price for that month (conservative: CIF is below wholesale). Effect: tomato price 1.29-1.31 -> 1.23 GBP/kg, revenue -33 to -70 k/yr, NPV 3.5 % about -0.6 to -1.3 M; cucumber +0.01 GBP/kg (December).
