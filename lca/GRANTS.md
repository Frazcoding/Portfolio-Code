# Grants screen (BP24d, 2026-10-07; Firecrawl, excerpts; verify each scheme's rules before relying on it)

| Scheme | Finding | Fit for a glasshouse |
|---|---|---|
| Food and Drink Processing Scheme Scotland (FDPSS), Scottish Government GBP 9 M package, administered by Scotland Food & Drink; opened Spring 2026 | grants up to GBP 400,000 (gov.scot news page, modified 17 Mar 2026); intervention rate, eligible costs and closing date not found on that page | Processing, not primary production: a packhouse might qualify, the glasshouse would not. Terms to read on Scotland Food & Drink |
| Food Processing, Marketing and Co-operation (FPMC) | Rural Payments: not running in 2023/24 (Scottish budget pressure); NFU Scotland asks for GBP 26 M for FFIS and FPMC in the 2026-27 budget | Same: marketing / processing. Closed or uncertain |
| SMART: SCOTLAND (Scottish Enterprise) | on hold; check again late autumn 2026 | R&D / innovation projects, not capex |
| Farming Equipment and Technology Fund (FETF), Defra | 2026 round closed 28 Apr 2026, GBP 50 M, 40-50 % of item cost; last round in its current form, revamped for 2027 | England only. Not available in Scotland |
| Scottish Government Small Producers Pilot Fund | GBP 0.5 M in 2025/26 | Too small and aimed at small producers |
| GHISP (earlier finding) | closed, aimed at heat-technology makers | No |

Reading: no open grant was found that funds a glasshouse or its heat pump in Scotland. The one live route is the FDPSS for a
packhouse, capped at GBP 400,000 (about 4 % of a GBP 10 M plan) and only if the terms fit. The plan therefore carries no grant
in its central case. The "30-50 % capital match" in `../Market/02_SCOTLAND_GO_TO_MARKET_STRATEGY.md` stays unverified: FETF's
40-50 % is England-only and for equipment items. A grant's NPV gain is about its value at t0.
